# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the MyOS project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on technical ideas and behavior, not on people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect project scope and maintainers’ decisions; disagree professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or attention, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the contact method listed in the GitHub repository profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where practical, and may remove content or restrict participation based on the severity and context.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules consistently and communicate enforcement decisions respectfully. If a concern involves a maintainer, contact another repository owner or GitHub Support.
